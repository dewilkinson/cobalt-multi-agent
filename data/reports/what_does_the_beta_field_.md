In your core scanner, the **Beta** field measures a stock's volatility in relation to the overall market (typically the S&P 500). 

- **Beta = 1.0**: The stock moves in sync with the market.
- **Beta > 1.0**: The stock is more volatile than the market (e.g., a Beta of 1.5 suggests the stock might move 1.5% for every 1% move in the index).
- **Beta < 1.0**: The stock is less volatile than the market, often indicating a more defensive asset.
- **Negative Beta**: The stock tends to move in the opposite direction of the market (common with inverse ETFs or certain gold stocks).
