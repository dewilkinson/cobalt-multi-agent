**EXECUTIVE SECTOR ANALYSIS**

The semiconductor and autonomous technology sectors are entering a critical "verification phase" for FY2026. While the **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** expansion remains the primary liquidity driver, the market is shifting focus from "capacity promises" to "shipment execution." **ASML** and **TSMC** have signaled robust demand for high-end lithography and 2nm/3nm nodes, but structural bottlenecks in **CoWoS (Chip on Wafer on Substrate)** packaging remain a persistent headwind for top-line upside in H2 2026.

Geopolitically, the U.S. has implemented a "Managed Restriction" regime. The introduction of **volume caps** (1 million units/year) for **Nvidia H200** exports to China, combined with a **25% AI chip tariff**, has created a fragmented global pricing environment.
